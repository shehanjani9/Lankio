Lankio logo exploration

Three original directions for Lankio Digital Solutions, each shown on light and dark. Every letter and shape is hand-built vector geometry: no fonts, no gradients, no effects. Concept 01 is recommended and has a full export set below.

01

Frame L

Monogram

Recommended

A heavy L corner and a light opposite corner form an implied frame: a viewfinder crop mark, a screen viewport, a code bracket. The space between them is the work.

Strength
Strongest silhouette at 16 px · evolves the L already used on lankio.it · one accent, no effects
Watch
Relies on the pairing: the L alone is a simple letter
02

LANKiO

Wordmark

Custom geometric capitals with a single signature detail: the I carries a square pixel in electric blue, a nod to the lowercase i of the earlier LANKiO logo.

Strength
Most legible name · calm, international, timeless · works in one colour
Watch
No standalone symbol; the pixel alone is too small for a favicon
03

Spark

Signature

A square (structure, code) and a quarter disc (the idea rising) meet at a single point. Paired with a geometric lowercase wordmark and spaced tagline.

Strength
Friendliest of the three · distinct abstract symbol · violet gives a separate personality
Watch
The abstract symbol needs the name beside it to be read as Lankio
